About Me

Built on curiosity,
driven by the work.

I'm Braden Covington, a Construction Science & Management sophomore at Kansas State University, originally from Omaha, Nebraska. What drew me into construction is hard to put into a single sentence — it's the sheer scale of what the industry touches. Almost every sector of the economy requires construction at some point, which means the ceiling on where this career can go is essentially nonexistent.

But beyond the scope of the work, it's the people that keep me energized. The camaraderie in this industry is unlike anything I've experienced elsewhere — from classmates grinding through coursework together, to seasoned professionals in the field who are genuinely open to sharing what they've learned. Some of my closest friendships have come out of this program, and the industry connections I've made have only reinforced why I chose this path.

I've had the opportunity to work on mission-critical data center projects with two of the most respected names in construction, and I'm always looking for the next challenge that pushes me to grow.
